## Reddit post

Suggested title: **I built an open-source tool for exploring life decisions as paths and trade-offs — looking for feedback**

```text
I’ve been thinking about how most AI decision help ends at a single recommendation. I wanted something that makes the assumptions and trade-offs inspectable instead.

So I built Crossroads: an open-source personal decision harness. You start with a decision, explore possible paths, change a variable such as savings, and compare the consequences. It is not a prediction tool and it does not pick for you.

The public version is a keyless, fictional startup-career demo with five levels of choices. The source is open too:

Demo: https://decision-harness.vercel.app
GitHub: https://github.com/khanmubaris/decision-harness

I’d value feedback on two questions:
1. What would make a tool like this genuinely useful for a decision you are facing?
2. Which assumptions should it make easier to challenge?

I’m the builder and welcome criticism. I’ll follow the rules of any community before posting.
```

Post this once in the most relevant community after reading its rules. Do not cross-post identical promotional copy widely; tailor the opening paragraph to that community and participate in replies.

## Launch sequence

1. Publish the X post with the five-level-tree visual.
2. Reply to your own X post with the design principle: “The tool should show its assumptions, not hide them behind a recommendation.”
3. Post the Reddit version to one relevant community whose rules permit it. Lead with the discussion question, not the link.
4. Spend the next 24 hours replying to every substantive question and log recurring feedback as GitHub issues.
